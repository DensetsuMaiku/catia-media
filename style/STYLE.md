# Catia style guide

Working guide for Catia, an AI virtual singer. The world is Milan neon-noir industrial hyperpop. New stills and video match this guide and the reference images in `looks/` before they move to `finals/`.

## Palette

- **Neon pink** is the accent: light, type, and glitch edges.
- **Black** is the ground: large fields, crushed shadows, industrial surfaces.
- **Milan neon-noir** is the grade: night city, wet streets, pink neon eaten by black. The current approved look is this night grade.

Daylight is reserved for a later LoRA version. It is not an approved grade yet.

## Typography

- **Display.** Condensed industrial faces for titles and on-screen type. High contrast, short lines, readable against neon pink and black.
- **Text.** A plain grotesque for captions and any small type, readable at phone size.

List a specific family here before using it on a post-ready export. Until a family is listed, keep type limited to those two roles and skip decorative faces.

## Watermark

Approved exports carry the Catia watermark.

- Place it in a corner that leaves the face clear.
- Keep it readable on black and on neon pink.
- The master file will live in `style/` once it is approved. Until that file is here, ask before inventing a stand-in on anything in `finals/`.

## AI label

Every video post is labeled as AI-generated before it is scheduled.

- Use the platform AI-content control on X, TikTok, and Meta where that control exists.
- Put a plain disclosure in the caption: Catia is an AI virtual singer and the video is AI-generated.
- Add a short on-screen label (for example `AI-generated`) when the edit can hold it without covering the face or the watermark.

Stills follow the same disclosure when the caption would otherwise read as footage of a real person.
